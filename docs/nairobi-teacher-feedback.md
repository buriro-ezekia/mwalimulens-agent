# Nairobi teacher feedback protocol — synthetic walkthrough

**Purpose:** gather early practitioner feedback on whether MwalimuLens presents evidence
clearly enough to support a teacher's professional judgement. This is exploratory usability
feedback, **not** an educational impact evaluation or classroom validation.

## Preparation

Use only the fictional L001 / MATH-FRACTIONS case in the separate
[Nairobi teacher-review sandbox](nairobi-teacher-review.md). Show the original assessment
trend, EV-008 counter-observation, uncertainty and proposed teacher question. Do not show
real learner records or ask participants to describe identifiable learners.

Explain that the reviewer identity is simulated, the AI candidate is seeded for the
isolated review demo, and the prototype is not approved for classroom deployment.

## Short observation protocol (15–20 minutes)

1. Ask the practitioner to describe the learner's apparent progress in their own words.
2. Ask which evidence supports the candidate and which evidence challenges it.
3. Ask whether the uncertainty and suggested teacher question are useful.
4. Demonstrate approve, edit and reject in **separate disposable sandbox runs**.
5. Ask what evidence or safeguards would be needed before using this workflow in practice.

Do not coach the participant towards approving a candidate. Record what they actually
understand and where they hesitate. No real learner names, school identifiers or other
personal data are required.

## Interpretation and reporting

Report the number of practitioners approached and the number who provided feedback.
Summarise recurring themes, concrete usability problems and changes made. Do not infer
learning gains, improved teacher decisions, adoption rates or generalisability from a
small convenience sample. If no practitioner feedback has yet been obtained, state that
explicitly rather than inventing findings.

Keep feedback anonymous, avoid collecting sensitive information and obtain appropriate
permission before recording or publishing any quotations. Do not commit raw interview
notes containing personal information.

## Decision gate

Practitioner feedback may inform interface wording and evidence presentation. It must
not justify weakening the human review boundary or enabling real learner data without
separate authentication, privacy and school-governance review.
